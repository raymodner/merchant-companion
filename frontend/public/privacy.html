Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This policy explains what data Merchant Community Map collects, why, and how you can control it.

What we collect

  • Account data — your chosen username, email address, and a bcrypt-hashed password. Your plaintext password is never stored.
  • Map content — terrain paints, tribe markers, and settlement data you place on the map.
  • Preferences — your saved region preference (country / state) so the map opens in the right place when you log in.
  • Server logs — IP addresses and request timestamps, kept for up to 30 days for security and rate-limiting purposes.

Why we collect it

  • Account data is processed to provide and secure your account (legal basis: performance of a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • IP addresses / logs are processed to protect the service against abuse (legal basis: legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Cookies

We use exactly one cookie: an authentication token that keeps you logged in for up to 24 hours. It is set as HttpOnly and is strictly necessary for the service to function. No tracking, advertising, or analytics cookies are used.

Who we share data with

No one. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party. There are no analytics providers, ad networks, or CDN services that receive your data.

How long we keep it

Account data (username, email, password hash, preferences, and map content) is retained for as long as your account is active. Server logs are automatically deleted after 30 days. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of your personal data.
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate data.
  • Erasure — request deletion of your account and data.
  • Restriction — ask us to limit processing in certain circumstances.
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interest.

To exercise any of these rights, reach us via our Discord server. We will respond within 30 days.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch supervisory authority: Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the date above. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance.
